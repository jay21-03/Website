# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Customers browse Bau Truc pottery, manage carts, place online orders, view payment/order status, and request workshop bookings. Admin users manage catalog, inventory, orders, notifications, reporting, workshop offerings/bookings, and support contact information.

## Product Purpose

The system is an ecommerce backend and React storefront/admin console for Dang Xem Bau Truc pottery. It should make product discovery, checkout, payment tracking, and store administration reliable while preserving the frozen backend business rules.

## Brand Personality

Crafted, clear, grounded.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic marketplace visuals, fake shipping/payment flows, decorative admin dashboards, fetch-all client-side business filtering, and interfaces that imply unsupported features such as COD, vouchers, shipping tracking, or automatic refunds.

## Design Principles

- Keep commerce state authoritative from the backend.
- Make admin workflows dense, predictable, and hard to misuse.
- Show payment and order states separately and honestly.
- Preserve Bau Truc craft identity without reducing task clarity.
- Prefer clear empty/error/loading states over silent failure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, keyboard-accessible forms, readable contrast, reduced surprise motion, and responsive layouts for desktop and mobile Chromium-class browsers.
